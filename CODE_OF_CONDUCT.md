# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Nudg community and its projects will be treated with respect regardless of levels of experience, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- **Focusing on what is best** for the community
- **Showing empathy** towards other community members
- **Being inclusive** and welcoming to newcomers
- **Keeping discussions professional and evidence-based**, especially regarding health topics
- **Helping others learn** and grow as contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassing, insulting, or demeaning comments
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Unethical conduct or dishonesty
- Discrimination or exclusion based on protected characteristics
- Providing medical advice when not qualified to do so
- Misrepresenting health/clinical information

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, pull requests)
- Community spaces (forums, chat channels, events)
- Official project events
- Unofficial project-related events and social media

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to:

**Email:** conduct@nudg.com

**In Reports, Please Include:**
- Your contact information
- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- Context and witnesses
- Any relevant links or communications

Reports will be handled with confidentiality.

### Investigation
- All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly
- We will not publicly disclose reporter identities without consent
- We will respect the privacy of those involved
- We will work toward a resolution

### Consequences
Possible outcomes for violations include:
- Warning or request for apology
- Temporary ban from community spaces (7-30 days)
- Permanent ban from community and project participation
- Escalation to project governance if needed
